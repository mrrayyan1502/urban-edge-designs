# Urban Edge Design — 30-Day Growth Launch Plan

## Today — launch checklist
1. Deploy this source.
2. Open `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` directly in a browser.
3. In Google Search Console, inspect and request indexing once for:
   - `/ideas/small-living-room-ideas`
   - `/ideas/small-bedroom-ideas`
   - `/ideas/small-kitchen-ideas`
   - `/ideas/studio-apartment-ideas`
4. Test cookie consent: reject analytics, then accept analytics, and confirm GA4 Real-Time behaves accordingly.
5. Test desktop + mobile navigation, search, contact, newsletter fallback, Shop the Look, and a deliberate 404.

## Week 1 — indexing + internal authority
- Ensure all 20 articles are discoverable from category hubs and search.
- Request indexing only for priority pages; do not repeatedly submit the same URL.
- Add 3–5 contextual internal links whenever a new article is published.
- Review Search Console Page Indexing reports for canonical, noindex or crawl errors.

## Week 2 — Pinterest + Google Images
For every priority article:
- Create one 1000×1500 vertical Pinterest graphic.
- Use a descriptive Pin title and description.
- Use descriptive image filenames and alt text.
- Prefer approved/self-hosted WebP or AVIF assets rather than relying indefinitely on third-party image URLs.

Priority visual pages:
- Small Living Room Ideas
- Small Bedroom Ideas
- Small Kitchen Ideas
- Studio Apartment Ideas
- Small Apartment Storage Ideas
- Rental-Friendly Decorating Ideas

## Week 3 — commercial intent
- Apply to suitable home/furniture affiliate programmes.
- Replace generic retailer searches only with approved tracked links.
- Build Shop-the-Look collections around products you can verify.
- Keep affiliate disclosures prominent.

## Week 4 — Search Console decisions
Review:
- impressions
- clicks
- queries
- pages receiving impressions
- CTR
- average position

Expand topics that already receive impressions instead of publishing random articles.

2. Affiliate links to verified products
3. Email list
